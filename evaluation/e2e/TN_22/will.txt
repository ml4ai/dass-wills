LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF [Person-1] I, [Person-1], of Gates, Lauderdale County, Tennessee, being of sound and disposing mind and memory, do hereby make, publish, and declare this instrument as my LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT, hereby revoking all wills and codicils to wills heretofore made by me. ARTICLE I I direct that all of my just debts, my funeral expenses, and the costs of the administration of my estate be paid as soon as practicable after my death. ARTICLE II All of the property which I may own at the time of my death, real, personal and mixed, tangible and intangible, of whatsoever nature and wheresoever situated, including all property which I may acquire or become entitled to after the execution of this Will, I hereby give, devise and bequeath in fee to my granddaughter, [Person-2]. In the event that my said granddaughter does not survive me, then I hereby give, devise and bequeath said property to my great grandson, [Person-3] I am intentionally not leaving my other relatives anything under this will, not out of lack of love and affection, but because they have sufficient resources to provide for themselves. ARTICLE III I nominate and appoint my granddaughter, [Person-2], Executrix of this my LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT, and direct that no bond or sureties be required for the faithful performance of her duties. If my granddaughter shall predecease me, or for any reason shall fail to qualify as Executrix hereunder, or having qualified shall die or resign, then I appoint my great grandson, [Person-3], as substitute Executor, and direct that no bond or sureties be required for the faithful performance of his duties. I vest my said Executrix and substitute respectively with full power and authority to sell, transfer, encumber, mortgage, invest or otherwise dispose of any and all of my property, real or personal, which I may own at the time of my death, and to do every othej act and thing necessary or appropriate for the complete administration of my estate. IN TESTIMONY WHEREOF, 1 have hereunto subscribed my name this 7th day of April 1990, and do publish and declare this my LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT consisting of this and one (1) preceding typewritten pages, and for the purpose of identification I have initialed such page, in the presence of each and all of the subscribing witnesses whom I have requested to act as such by signing their names as attesting witnesses in my presence and in the presence of each other, and by signing the Affidavit below pursuant to the provisions of Tennessee Code Annotated 32-2-110. The foregoing instrument, consisting of this and one (1) preceding typewritten pages, was signed, sealed, published and declared by [Person-1], the Testatrix to be her LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT, in our presence, and we, at her request and in her presence, and in the presence of each other, have hereunto subscribed our names as witnesses, this 7th day of April, 1990, at Gates, Lauderdale County, Tennessee. AFFIDAVIT STATE OF TENNESSEE COUNTY OF LAUDERDALE We, the undersigned, being first duly sworn, make oath that [Person-1], on this 7th day of April, 1990, declared to us that this instrument is her LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT; that she then signed this instrument in our sight and presence, and in the sight and presence of each other, subscribe our names hereto as attesting witnesses; that at the time of execution the TESTARIX was more than 18 years of age, of sound mind and disposing memory and did not appear under any undue influence; and that the undersigned, each being more than 18 years of age, make and sign this Affidavit at the Testatrix's request on the day of date above written. Sworn to and subscribed before me on this, the 7th day of April, 1990. MY COMMISSION EXPIRES: 8-15-90
